# AcmeCorp Privacy and Security Policy

1. Deletion: when a customer asks to be deleted, we remove their data from ALL systems within 30 days.
2. Logs: we never write customer emails, phone numbers or card numbers into application logs.
3. Retention: we delete customer data 2 years after an account is closed.
4. Admins: every admin account must use multi-factor authentication (MFA).
5. Leavers: staff accounts are disabled within 24 hours of someone leaving.
